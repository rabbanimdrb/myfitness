Do's & Don'ts

CRITICAL RULES
✓

Always Do This

  • Warm up 5–10 min before every session (arm circles, hip circles, jumping jacks)
  • Control the movement — go slow on the way down (3 seconds) for more muscle
  • Full range of motion — chest to floor on push-ups, deep on squats
  • Add reps or harder variations every week — this is how you grow
  • Drink 2.5–3 litres of water daily
  • Sleep 7–9 hours — muscles are built during rest, not training
  • Do 10 min flexibility routine daily (keep that mobility)
  • Eat protein within 30–45 min after training
✗

Never Do This

  • Skip rest days — overtraining kills progress and injures you
  • Rush through reps for high numbers — slow controlled reps = more muscle
  • Train through shoulder pain — stop immediately if sharp pain occurs
  • Skip leg day — legs trigger most testosterone, skipping slows overall growth
  • Do overhead pressing movements if shoulder hurts (pike push-ups, military press)
  • Eat less than 100g protein daily — you'll lose muscle instead of building it
  • Train the same muscle two days in a row — muscles need 48h to recover
  • Compare your 2-week progress to someone's 2-year body
⚠️

Shoulder Issues — Avoid These Completely

  • Overhead pressing (pain-free range only)
  • Wide-grip dips (elbows flared out)
  • Behind-the-neck movements
  • Wall handstands (skip until healed)
  • Heavy upright rows
  • Lateral raises above shoulder height
  • Sleeping on painful shoulder

Instead: Do scapular push-ups, face pulls, and band external rotations to strengthen and rehab the shoulder safely.
